# Knuckle and rocker mount: interface for a printed or a metal part

Generated by `tools/interface_doc.py` from `parts/swap_interface.py`, the one place these numbers live; the printed parts are built from them.
Owner (November 24): "printed + servo based on actual production off the shelf parts and measurements JIC i want to swap it out later".

How to swap: the metal knuckle and the metal rocker bracket are the printed parts' shapes, machined (aluminium 6082 or 7075, or steel). Export the
part from the model (`soma export "Front knuckle 1" --format step`, `"Rocker bracket 1"`; the left ones are mirror images) and order it as drawn,
with the threads and fits below. Every hole, face and the outer envelope then match, so nothing else changes. Strength of the printed parts: NOTES.md.

Off-the-shelf metal parts: none is made for this layout (leading swing arm with a translating carrier, kingpin at x 123 inside a 1.9 in rim,
rocker under the arm pivot); RC knuckles and C-hubs are for solid axles or wishbones and do not share these hard points. Nothing was found
that fits, so the interface is for a machined part. (Not an exhaustive market search.)

Bought parts at these interfaces, with the maker's dimensions and sources: `bought_part_datasheets_2026-11-24.md`.
